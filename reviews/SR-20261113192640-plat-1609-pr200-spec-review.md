## Summary

Ticket: PLAT-1609. A spec review of two amendments. The FR-037 behaviour bullet and FR-037-AC-2 now let a DOCX made under the pandoc `--sandbox` of FR-032 hold the Unix epoch. FR-042-AC-5 now says what a headerless fixture is. The sub-analyses I chose for this scope are normative wording and consistency with the neighbouring FRs. `quire validate --scope .` on both files exits 0; the only diagnostic is a module notice.

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL**: two low consistency findings. The ruling holds up. I reproduced that pandoc 3.14 under `--sandbox` stamps 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z whatever `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` says. The wall-clock exclusion ("SHALL NOT hold the export time", "none is within a day of the test's wall-clock time") is kept, and PDF dates must still equal the commit time. Neither amendment quotes an external standard.
